THE PROBLEM & THE SOLUTION

Why Fruit Ripeness Matters to Every
Household

Picking fruit at the grocery store or timing consumption at home shouldn't be guesswork.

⌕

The Common Dilemma

Consumers struggle to discern inner ripeness strictly from outer appearance. Thick skins, deceptive wax coatings, and subtle color shifts lead to two disappointing extremes:

  • Cutting fruit too early: firm, flavorless fruit that isn't ready to enjoy.
  • Waiting too long: fruit passes its ideal eating window.
  • Grocery store uncertainty: choosing without knowing what to expect.
✳

The PickPerfect Solution

PickPerfect pairs everyday smartphone or laptop cameras with computer vision machine learning. In seconds, the model evaluates the image and returns a class prediction:

  • Objective classification: one of the model's three labels.
  • Confidence transparency: see the score for every class.
  • Actionable guidance: practical next steps for your fruit.
MODEL STANDARD

Our Three Ripeness Categories

PickPerfect uses the three labels configured in your Teachable Machine model.

CLASS 01🌱

Unripe

Fruit that has not yet reached its ideal eating stage. It may be firm and still developing its characteristic flavor.

↗ Guidance: Give it more time to ripen.
CLASS 02🍐

Ripe

Fruit at or near its ideal eating stage, with the expected color, softness, and flavor for that fruit.

↗ Guidance: Enjoy it while it's ready.
CLASS 03🍂

Overripe

Fruit that may be past its preferred texture or eating window. Check it before deciding whether to use or discard it.

↗ Guidance: Check quality; compost if no longer usable.
